How the math works

The spectrogram

A Fourier transform turns each 46 ms slice of sound into its frequencies. Stack the slices and steady notes draw horizontal lines while drum hits draw vertical ones.

Drums

A median filter running up each column keeps short, wide bursts and erases steady tones. Whatever survives is percussion.

Bass

Of the steady tones, everything under about 120 Hz goes to bass, fading out by 260 Hz.

Vocals

Lead vocals are mixed dead center, so they're identical in both speakers. Tones in the voice range where left ≈ right go here.

Other

Everything left: synths, guitars, pads, and anything panned to the sides. The four stems always add back up to the original exactly.

Tempo

The drum stem's energy jumps give an onset curve. Autocorrelation finds the gap that repeats most strongly, and that gap is the beat.
